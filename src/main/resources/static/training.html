Disaster Safety Guidance

Flood

  • Move to higher ground immediately
  • Avoid walking through moving water
  • Follow evacuation routes
  • Keep emergency kit ready

Earthquake

  • Drop, Cover, Hold under a sturdy table
  • Stay away from windows and heavy objects
  • Move to open area after shaking stops
  • Expect aftershocks

Fire

  • Use stairs instead of elevators
  • Cover nose and mouth with cloth
  • Exit through nearest safe route
  • Feel doors before opening

Cyclone

  • Stay indoors and away from windows
  • Follow official alerts
  • Move to safe shelter if required
  • Secure loose objects outside

Emergency Preparedness Checklist

Keep these essential items ready for any disaster situation:

Drinking water (3-day supply)
Flashlight with extra batteries
First aid kit
Portable phone charger
Important documents
Non-perishable food
Essential medications
Blankets or sleeping bags
Multi-tool or basic tools
Emergency contact list
