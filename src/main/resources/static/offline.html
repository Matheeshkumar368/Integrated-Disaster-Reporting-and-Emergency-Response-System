Offline Survival Directives
Flood / Flash Water: Move to the highest ground immediately. Never walk, swim, or drive through flowing water. Stay clear of submerged power lines.
Cyclone / Storm Surge: Stay indoors on the leeward side of the building. Disconnect electrical mains. Keep mobile battery conserved.
Earthquake: Drop, Cover, and Hold on under sturdy furniture. Protect head and neck. Do not run outside while shaking continues.
