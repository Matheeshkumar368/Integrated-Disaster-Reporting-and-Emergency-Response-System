Flood Safety Guidelines
  • Do not walk or drive through moving flood waters ("Turn Around, Don't Drown").
  • Move essential valuables, medicine, and food to upper floors in inundation zones.
  • Disconnect electrical appliances and main switches before water enters premises.
  • Drink boiled or bottled water to prevent water-borne disease outbreaks.
Cyclone & Severe Storm Protocol
  • Secure loose exterior objects (tins, roofs, outdoor furniture, solar panels).
  • Keep battery-powered emergency radios, torches, and power banks charged.
  • Stay indoors away from windows during the passage of the storm center ("eye").
  • Follow official evacuation orders issued by State Disaster Authorities.
Earthquake Action Steps
  • DROP, COVER, HOLD ON: Drop under a sturdy desk or table.
  • Stay away from glass windows, exterior walls, and falling lighting fixtures.
  • Do not use elevators during tremors; use staircase exits once shaking stops.
  • If outdoors, move safely away from power lines, poles, and high-rise facades.
Fire Hazard Emergency Procedure
  • Crawl low under smoke; cleaner air is closer to the floor.
  • Feel doors with the back of your hand before opening; do not open warm doors.
  • Dial 101 or 112 immediately once safely outside the burning structure.
  • Never re-enter a burning building for pets or personal possessions.
